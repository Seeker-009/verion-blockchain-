# VERION CONSENSUS COMPARISON
## Draft Version 0.1

### 1. Introduction

Consensus allows blockchain participants to agree on valid transactions and blocks.

### 2. Proof of Stake (PoS)

How it works:
- Validators stake cryptocurrency.
- Validators participate in block confirmation.
- Protocol rules may penalize dishonest behavior.

Advantages:
- Lower energy consumption.
- No specialized mining hardware required.
- Potentially efficient transaction processing.

Challenges:
- Stake concentration.
- Validator security.
- Complex reward and penalty rules.

### 3. Proof of Work (PoW)

How it works:
- Miners use computing power to solve cryptographic puzzles.
- The winning miner proposes a block.
- Other participants verify it.

Advantages:
- Established security model.
- Participation does not require staking coins.

Challenges:
- High energy consumption.
- Mining hardware costs.
- Mining power concentration.

### 4. Comparison

| Factor | PoS | PoW |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | Generally lower | Generally higher |
| Hardware | Standard servers | Mining hardware |
| Security | Economic stake | Computing power |
| Rewards | Staking | Mining |

### 5. VERION Evaluation Criteria

- Security.
- Decentralization.
- Operating costs.
- Performance.
- Validator accessibility.
- Long-term sustainability.

### 6. Preliminary Direction

Proof of Stake is the leading candidate.

### 7. Final Decision

Consensus mechanism: NOT YET SELECTED.
